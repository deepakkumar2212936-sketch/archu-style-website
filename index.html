About Archu Style

Meet your teacher

5+years of
experience

Learn from a teacher with more than 5 years of hands-on experience in stitching and clothing design. She has worked at several places, sharpening her craft along the way, and has taught many girls to stitch with confidence.

Her classes are simple, practical and friendly. You learn by doing, from your very first stitch to a finished outfit, with personal attention in a small batch.

  • Beginner to advanced classes
  • Practical, hands-on learning
  • Personal guidance in small batches

How booking works

Three easy steps to reserve your seat

  1. 1

    Choose your class

    Pick Basic Stitching, Blouse & Kurti Cutting or Advanced Designing. Class timing is 11 AM to 3 PM.

  2. 2

    Fill your details

    Add your name, mobile number, address, age and gender. Ask us a question if you have one.

  3. 3

    Confirm your seat

    Tap confirm, then send your details to us on WhatsApp. We will tell you the fee and next steps.
